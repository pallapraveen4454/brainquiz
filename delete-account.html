BrainQuizAI Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their BrainQuizAI account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion, email us using the support contact provided in the BrainQuizAI app, or contact the developer through the app's Help & Support section. Include the email address associated with your BrainQuizAI account.

Data deleted

  • Firebase authentication account
  • User profile information
  • Quiz progress and achievement data
  • Leaderboard-related user data stored by BrainQuizAI
  • Locally stored app data on the user's device

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate record-keeping purposes. Such information will be deleted when the applicable retention requirement ends.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days after the request is verified.

Contact

Please use the Contact Support option inside the BrainQuizAI app to submit an account deletion request.
