BrainQuizAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

BrainQuizAI ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the BrainQuizAI mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information: email address, authentication details, username, and profile information when you create an account or sign in.
  • Google Sign-In information: information provided by Google during authentication, such as your email address and basic account identifier.
  • Guest account information: information required to maintain your guest progress and app session.
  • Gameplay and progress information: quiz answers, scores, XP, coins, streaks, achievements, category progress, leaderboard information, and related app activity.
  • Support information: information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for help.
  • Technical and advertising information: device or app-related information, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and approximate usage information that may be processed by advertising or platform service providers.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • authenticate users through email/password or Google Sign-In;
  • save quiz progress, achievements, rewards, streaks, and leaderboard data;
  • provide daily quizzes, app features, hints, and rewards;
  • respond to support requests;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve app reliability;
  • display and measure advertisements where applicable; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

3. Services and Third-Party Providers

BrainQuizAI may use trusted third-party services to provide app functionality. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

  • Firebase / Google services: may be used for authentication, cloud data storage, synchronization, and app infrastructure. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: may be used to display banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements. AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interaction information. See Google Partner Policies.
  • Google Gemini or other AI services: if AI-powered features are enabled, relevant requests may be processed by the applicable AI service to provide the requested feature. We do not ask users to submit passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information into AI prompts.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, support, or improve the app, or when required by law.

Certain information, such as username, avatar, XP, rank, or score, may be visible to other users through leaderboard or social-style app features when those features are enabled.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Guest data may be retained only as needed for the guest experience and may be lost when the guest session or app data is removed.

6. Account Deletion

Registered users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information through the account deletion process provided by BrainQuizAI. Account deletion may remove the Firebase account, associated cloud profile data, and locally stored app data, subject to information that must be retained by law.

To request account deletion, visit: BrainQuizAI Account Deletion

7. Children's Privacy

BrainQuizAI is an educational quiz application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to help protect information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional information, stop using the app, request account deletion, or contact us regarding questions about your information. Some features may not work properly without information necessary for account authentication or synchronization.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
